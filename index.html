THE DECISION ON ONE PAGE

What the CTO is being asked to sign.

    1 · LOCAL-FIRST VS THE CORPORATE MODEL

    Own the data, own the compute — and own the work.

    The corporate model rents intelligence per seat and per token from a vendor's cloud. Local-first runs open models on the laptops the company already owns and a small rack of its own inference nodes. Neither is free; they fail differently and they are governed differently.

    Local-firstCorporate (seats + cloud APIs)
    2 · ARCHITECTURE

    Ten layers, one boundary.

    Everything that touches personal data runs inside the company's network. The only door out is for fetching model weights, which carry no personal data.

    7 · GO / NO-GO

    Each deployment decision, gated on the evidence — and sealed.

    Every decision is a dual-map case: the positive map (what the evidence supports) beside a negative map (the evidence-supported ways it fails). One severe, evidence-backed failure BLOCKs and no amount of positive evidence buys it back; an unmeasured risk holds it at PENDING, never a block. The evidence is fallfloor's receipted run; the thresholds are this design's policy, and you can change them.
