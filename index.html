7 · RECEIPTS

Each deployment decision, gated on measured evidence — and sealed.

Every decision is a dual-map case: the positive map (what the evidence supports) beside a negative map (the evidence-supported ways it fails). One severe, evidence-backed failure BLOCKs and no amount of positive evidence buys it back; an unmeasured risk holds it at PENDING, never a block. The evidence is fallfloor's receipted run; the thresholds are this design's policy, and you can change them.
